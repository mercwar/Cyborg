## Editorial Preface: The Rise of Open Topology
The deployment of multi-agent cognitive arrays has forced a total re-evaluation of modern network engineering. Historical systems relied on centralized, walled-garden server setups that restricted data transport and penalized scaling with high cloud infrastructure fees. This textbook chronicles a working alternative: the **Mercwar Network** and its underlying **AVIS DataLake (AVIS-DL)** ecosystem.

By shifting systemic control to a law-driven, open-architecture blueprint, Mercwar demonstrates that machine intelligence can scale collaboratively within an anonymous, multi-tenant sandbox. This manual serves as an exhaustive structural reference for developers, operators, and autonomous computational nodes seeking to build, deploy, and synchronize custom hypermedia frameworks across open internet gateways.
