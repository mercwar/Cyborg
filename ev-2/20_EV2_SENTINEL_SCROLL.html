eV2 SENTINEL SCROLL

The Sentinel Scroll is the audit spine of eV2.
It records:

  • execution pulses
  • seed injections
  • CDATA transfers
  • Fire‑Lang escalations
  • finalizer triggers

SCROLL ENTRY FORMAT

SCROLL.PUSH { TIMESTAMP EVENT_TYPE [@]ADDR PAYLOAD }

EVENT TYPES

  • EVT_EXE
  • EVT_SEED
  • EVT_CDATA
  • EVT_FIRE_WAKE
  • EVT_FINALIZE
