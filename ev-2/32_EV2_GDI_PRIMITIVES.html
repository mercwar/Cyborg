eV2 GDI PRIMITIVES

GDI primitives define the drawing operations available to EV2.

PRIMITIVES

  • GDI.LINE
  • GDI.RECT
  • GDI.TEXT
  • GDI.FILL
  • GDI.CLEAR

PRIMITIVE STRUCT

begin.spec EV2_GDI_COMMAND { [#]CMD_TYPE [#]X1 [#]Y1 [#]X2 [#]Y2 {STR}TEXT } end.spec

EXAMPLE

GDI.LINE 10 10 200 10
